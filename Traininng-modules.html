4. Winter Term Curriculum & Professional Certifications

During the winter term, the program splits focus between indoor propagation facilities and technical telemetry prep, while embedding national standards to certify participants as Firefighter Type 2 (FFT2).

Greenhouse & Native Nursery Propagation Track

Hands-on training in seed cleaning, scarification, soil science, mycorrhizal inoculation, and controlled-environment microclimate management for native conifers and riparian starts.

Aerial Drone Mapping & GIS Track (FAA Part 107)

Rigorous study of FAA regulations, airspace classifications, automated grid flight telemetry programming using RGB and thermal sensors, and offline GIS burn-scar evaluation.

NWCG Wildland Firefighter Certification (FFT2)

Standardized interagency credentialing incorporating IS-100 (Incident Command), S-190 (Fire Behavior), S-130 (Firefighter Training & Field Day), L-180 (Human Factors), and the 45-pound Arduous Pack Test.
