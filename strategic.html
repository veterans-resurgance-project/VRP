🌲 Strategic Collaboration Framework

The Veterans Resurgence Project, in tandem with Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC, deploys disciplined, safety-certified veteran cohorts to solve regional environmental crises while building workforce pipelines across Linn County, Oregon.

Choose your sector below to view operational parameters, mutual benefits, and legal frameworks, or to download necessary onboarding forms.

📁 Commercial Logging & Timber

What We Require: Direct access to live commercial acreage for rigorous, hands-on field conditioning and early apprenticeship training. Requires a signed, renewable one-year MOU.

What You Gain: A structured crew operating under an 0800-hour military cohort framework. You get disciplined manpower to clear slash, manage site preparation, and cut brush at zero wage-risk, providing a first-look recruitment pipeline at top performers.

Firewall: Logging partners act strictly as non-voting Technical Advisory Board members to completely eliminate non-profit board conflicts of interest.

📁 Private Land Owners & Watersheds

What We Require: Entry permits and land-access clearances to wildfire-damaged acreage, degraded hillsides, and critical river boundaries requiring urgent erosion management.

What You Gain: Free post-wildfire environmental remediation, hazardous fuel reduction, and professional land stabilization executed by a disciplined workforce operating under an OSU Extension-backed research protocol.

Threshold: Standard defensible space clearing is capped at 16–24 free hours before transitioning to structured Cascadia LLC commercial terms.

📁 Nurseries & Propagation Outfits

What We Require: Technical partnership tracks tailored for disabled or less physically agile veterans. We require dedicated greenhouse space or nursery allocations to run native seed propagation tracks.

What You Gain: Consistent, highly disciplined workspace support to optimize your commercial crop management, plant care, inventory logistics, and health-tracking operations.
