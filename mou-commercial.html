Industrial Operations & Trade-for-Work MOU

Commercial Logging & Fire Contractor Framework Agreement (Including 250-Hour BOLI Compliance Addendum)

1. Purpose & Scope

This Memorandum of Understanding establishes a non-financial asset-sharing and trade-for-work framework between Watership Stewards and participating commercial logging and forestry contractors operating within Linn County and the Willamette Valley.

2. Operational Value Proposition

"Here is how our project benefits your bottom line: We absorb all the upfront risk, screening, and basic training costs." We take local veterans, put them through our 0800 morning muster, drill them on site safety, pack-test them for wildland fitness, and put them to work on basic fuels reduction and chainsaw maintenance. When you subcontract our crew at $24.50 an hour, you aren't just getting dependable labor—you are getting an active look at your next full-time hire.

3. BOLI Apprenticeship & 250-Hour Cap

Under our BOLI framework model, we strictly cap individual deployments at 250 hours with us on a commercial site. If a crew member stands out and fits your crew culture, you are encouraged to hire them directly with zero finders' fees and zero red tape.
