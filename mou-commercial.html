Partner Briefing: Commercial Logging & Fire Contractors

"Here is how our project benefits your bottom line: We absorb all the upfront risk, screening, and basic training costs."

We take local veterans, put them through our 0800 morning muster, drill them on site safety, pack-test them for wildland fitness, and put them to work on basic fuels reduction and chainsaw maintenance. When you subcontract our crew at $24.50 an hour, you aren't just getting dependable, drug-free labor on your tract—you are getting an active look at your next full-time hire.

Under our BOLI apprenticeship model, we cap guys at 300 hours with us on a commercial site. If one of our guys stands out and fits your crew culture, we want you to hire him away. There are zero finders' fees and zero red tape. We promote our next trained man from the nursery up to our travel crew, and you get a vetted, hard-working employee who already knows your equipment and standards.
