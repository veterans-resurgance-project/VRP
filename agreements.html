Partners, Agreements & Contracts

Standardized legal frameworks, operational charters, and service permits governing our community service windows, landowner waivers, and commercial firewalls.

1. Technical Advisory Board Charter & Firewall Covenant

Formalizes the participation of industry advisors in Linn County (Waterloo Park Hub) while strictly protecting the project from board control issues.

Key Governance Constraints:

  • 0% Voting Control: Advisors hold no voting rights, corporate governance authority, or fiduciary control over the Non-Profit board.
  • Advisory Capacity Only: Safety, technical forestry, and logging standards input is strictly advisory.
  • Conflict Mitigation: Advisors cannot influence financial allocations or subcontracts between VRP and Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC.

2. Landowner Access Permit & Liability Waiver

Handed to private landowners before tools hit the ground for the 16-to-24 hour free brush-clearing safety windows.

Key Operational Clauses:

  • Right-of-Entry: Grants mobile crews permission to execute wildland fire defense clearing and watershed erosion management.
  • 100% Free Tier & No Tips Policy: Strictly prohibits cash tips or service fees; excess work transitions to Cascadia LLC.
  • Liability Release: Landowners waive claims for accidental property damage or operational liabilities during volunteer land management sweeps.

3. Request for Service & Commercial Subcontract Bid Form

Used when a client’s request exceeds the 24-hour free community cap, transferring the scope over to a paid commercial contract under Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC.

Commercial Rate Structure:

  • Standard Labor Rate: $24.50 per hour per crew member (Subject to 250-Hour Apprenticeship Cap rules).
  • Heavy Equipment: Flat-rate quote applied based on machine footprint requirements (Excavator / Skidding Supports).
  • Eligible Scopes: Commercial timber harvesting, hillslope stabilization, post-wildfire remediation, and multi-acre hazardous fuels reduction.
