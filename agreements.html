1. Technical Advisory Board Charter & Firewall Covenant

Location Base: Linn County, Oregon (Waterloo Park Hub)

1. Purpose & Scope

The Technical Advisory Board (TAB) exists solely to provide industry-specific safety guidelines, technical forestry oversight, and tactical advice regarding commercial logging standards and equipment deployment to the Veterans Resurgence Project.

2. Structural Firewall & Non-Voting Status

By signing below, the Technical Advisor explicitly acknowledges and agrees to the following governance constraints:

  • 0% Voting Control: The Advisor holds no voting rights, no corporate governance authority, and no fiduciary control over the Non-Profit (501c3 candidate) board of directors.
  • Advisory Capacity Only: All input regarding field operations, sawyer training, or safety metrics is strictly advisory.
  • Conflict of Interest Mitigation: The Advisor shall not vote on, influence, or direct any financial distribution, grant fund allocation, or commercial subcontracting decisions made between the non-profit entity and Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC.

3. Term & Termination

This agreement is effective for a period of one (1) year from the date of execution and may be renewed annually or terminated by either party with a written 30-day notice.

2. Sector 2 — Landowner Access Permit & Liability Waiver

Free Community Service Tier — 16-to-24 Hour Maximum Cap

1. Right-of-Entry Permit

The undersigned Landowner hereby grants permission to the mobile crews and field operators of the Veterans Resurgence Project to enter the property located at the address below for the purpose of executing wildland fire defense clearing, hazardous brush reduction, and watershed erosion management.

2. Service Threshold & Fees

  • 100% Free Tier: This tactical service is provided entirely free of charge as part of a veteran vocational training and community safety initiative.
  • No Tips/Cash Policy: Crews are strictly prohibited from accepting cash compensation, tips, or service fees from property owners.
  • Operational Cap: Free services are strictly limited to a maximum cap of 16-to-24 crew hours. Any work extending beyond this window or requiring heavy machinery extraction must transition to a separate commercial subcontract via Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC.

3. Liability Release & Waiver

The Landowner agrees to waive all claims against the Veterans Resurgence Project, its crew leads, trainees, and affiliates for any accidental property damage, personal injury, or operational liabilities that may occur during the execution of these volunteer land management services.
