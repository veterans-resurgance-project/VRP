Private Industry Accord

Commercial Logging & Forestry Contractors

Collaboration Overview

Active partnerships with regional commercial timber management systems providing crew deployments with heavy logging machinery access, technical tree hazard assessment oversight, and clear corporate employment paths.

How the Agreement Works

Operates via non-financial trade-for-work logistics. Veteran units provide skilled field support (including slash clearing, line management, and river anchorages) in direct exchange for trade mechanics training. Cap is strictly limited to 250 hours per unit to stay in clean compliance with BOLI requirements.

Governing Framework Instrument: Industrial Operations & Trade-for-Work MOU (w/ 250-Hour BOLI Addendum) Active File
