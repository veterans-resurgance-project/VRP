Liability Waivers & Winter Startup Framework

Internal operational protocol governing statutory risk protection, participant liability releases, and capital allocation matrices for the winter term launch across Ag and Timber/VRP tracks.

1. Participant Liability & Waiver Protocols

Track A: Ag & Micro-Farms Waiver

  • Statutory Integration: Aligned with Oregon’s Agritourism Liability Act (ORS 30.673), acknowledging inherent agricultural terrain and equipment risks. Requires mandatory physical warning signage at property entry points.
  • Operational Scope: Covers manual labor, hand tool handling (loppers, shovels), compost management, and interaction with partner homestead infrastructure.
  • Release Scope: Protects both the non-profit entity and the senior/legacy landowner from ordinary negligence claims while enforcing strict adherence to safety rules.

Track B: Timber & Watershed Restoration Waiver (VRP)

  • High-Risk Classification: Explicitly covers severe outdoor hazards including uneven post-wildfire terrain, falling limbs, heavy brush clearing, and chainsaw operations. Excluded from standard recreational immunity.
  • Medical & Evacuation Release: Acknowledges that the program does not provide primary health insurance; participant assumes responsibility for emergency transport costs.
  • Mandatory PPE & Compliance Clause: Ties active participation directly to mandatory safety gear enforcement (hard hats, eye protection, chainsaw chaps). Non-compliance results in immediate crew dismissal.

2. Winter Term Startup Capital & Resource Matrix

Operational Track Immediate Capital & Resource Needs Primary Purpose
Administrative & Legal 501(c)(3) filing fees, state incorporation costs, custom waiver drafting, CGL insurance down payment. Securing legal entity status and baseline risk protection prior to cohort arrival.
Ag & Micro-Farms Hand tools (loppers, pruners, shovels), wheelbarrows, drip-irrigation kits, high-vis vests, work gloves, safety signage. Equipping weekend crews for senior homesteader interventions and orchard rehabilitation.
Timber & Watershed (VRP) Heavy-duty PPE (chaps, hard hats), chainsaws, brush cutters, drone hardware/software package, Part 107 licensing, nursery media. Powering technical training tracks, aerial mapping, and post-wildfire watershed work.
Logistics & Transport Fuel stipends or dedicated transport solutions for moving crews to rural field locations. Ensuring reliable transit between classroom sessions and remote work plots.
