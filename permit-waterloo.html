Public Land Stewardship & Hazard Reduction MOU

Government & Agency Land Management Permit Framework (Waterloo & Regional Parks)

1. Authorization & Agency Coordination

This agreement outlines the direct coordination protocols between Watership Stewards and state/local public land management authorities (including Lane County Parks and regional municipal entities) for authorized hazard reduction, invasive species clearing, and public land restoration projects.

2. Safety Protocols & Liability Framework

All field units operate under strict safety compliance guidelines, incorporating standardized morning musters, personal protective equipment (PPE) mandates, and designated tool-handling protocols. Liability parameters and equipment-sharing frameworks are pre-cleared to ensure seamless multi-agency operations without administrative friction.

3. Designated Deployment Zones

Field restoration activities are mapped directly to approved park boundaries and public land corridors (such as Waterloo Park and designated county timber tracts), balancing ecological recovery with public safety access.
