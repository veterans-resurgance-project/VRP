Our organization operates structured W-2 veteran crews specializing in hazardous fuels reduction, public land stewardship, and watershed restoration. In addition to field operations, we are actively building out our native plant propagation and reforestation tracks to supply regional riparian and habitat recovery projects. To make this sustainable, we are seeking to explore a cooperative trade or shared-use arrangement for winter greenhouse space, where our veteran crew members can provide labor, site maintenance, or operational support in exchange for growing capacity. Furthermore, we are eager to align our teams with recognized educational frameworks—such as OSU's Home Horticulture, Plant Propagation, and PNW Native Plant short courses or certificate pathways—to ensure our veterans gain formal, verifiable credentials in soil science, botany, and pest management. We see strong potential for collaboration in the following areas: Winter Greenhouse Propagation & Trade: Collaborating on shared-use or trade-based greenhouse access for native plant cultivation and horticultural training. Workforce Upskilling & Certifications: Integrating science-based extension coursework and horticultural training into our veteran professional development pipeline. Aerial Monitoring & Field Support: Utilizing our FAA Part 107 sUAS (drone) capabilities for vegetative monitoring, canopy health tracking, and post-wildfire habitat assessments. We would welcome the opportunity to connect with your horticulture and forestry agents, visit the Tangent facility for a brief conversation, or host your team at our Waterloo Park Hub to discuss how we can build regional capacity together.
