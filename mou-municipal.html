Heavy Equipment & Resource Exchange

  • Partner-Provided Assets: Contractors and private timber firms periodically loan or stage heavy machinery (such as skidders, masticators, and excavators) for localized training maneuvers on approved deployment sites.
  • Reciprocal Labor: Veteran field units perform manual, labor-intensive forestry tasks that complement mechanized operations—such as manual slash piling, hand-line construction, stream-bank stabilization, and pre-commercial thinning.

Operational Safety & Logistics

  • Compliance Standards: Field maneuvers operate under strict industrial forestry safety standards, matching commercial logging protocols to ensure seamless coordination when working adjacent to or on active timber company lands.
  • Fiduciary Separation: Machinery use and contractor relationships are strictly bound by non-financial equipment-sharing agreements to prevent commercial co-mingling or liability bleed.
