Non-Financial Cooperative Research & Demonstration Agreement

Academic & Extension Service Partnership Framework

1. Purpose & Collaborative Scope

This agreement governs non-financial research partnerships between Watership Stewards and participating academic extension services, soil and water conservation districts, and educational institutions focused on post-wildfire watershed restoration in Linn County.

2. Resource & Labor Exchange Protocol

Under this asset-light framework, project field units provide hands-on volunteer and trainee labor for winter greenhouse propagation, native plant cultivation, and field data collection. In direct exchange, academic partners supply technical soil advisory support, lab analysis, and collaborative drone-mapping telemetry data.

3. Data Sharing & Intellectual Stewardship

All environmental telemetry, soil health metrics, and vegetative recovery data gathered across deployment sites are shared transparently to inform regional watershed health initiatives while maintaining strict respect for institutional research guidelines.
