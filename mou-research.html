Partnership Structure

A non-financial, research-focused cooperative framework established between The Veteran Resurgence Project (operating as The Watershed Stewards) and the Oregon State University Extension Service, specifically aligned with Linn County programs.

Core Resource & Labor Exchange

  • Project Contribution: Provides zero-cost veteran labor units to support winter greenhouse propagation, native plant cultivation, and on-site manual maintenance for agroforestry and restoration trials.
  • Extension Contribution: Delivers technical training modules, collaborative drone mapping data sharing, soil science advisory support, and Continuing Education Unit (CEU) accreditation tracking for field workshops.

Research & Monitoring Alignment

Integrates veteran field deployments directly with OSU Extension research goals by utilizing project work crews to monitor post-wildfire slope stability, track watershed sedimentation rates, and collect localized erosion data across test plots in the Willamette Valley and Linn County.

The Watership Stewards

Building transparent, data-backed resilience across Willamette Valley watersheds.
