Partners, Agreements & Contracts

Standardized legal frameworks, sample agreements, and compliance documents governing our community service windows, land access, and commercial firewalls.

1. Small-Job Community Service Agreement

Utilized for private property owners receiving our free community service window. This single-page document explicitly establishes operational boundaries and asset sourcing.

Key Provisions Included:

  • The 16–24 Hour Safety Window: Caps the maximum free labor deployment on any single residential or small private parcel for defensible space clearing and flash-fuel reduction.
  • "No Surcharges" Declaration: Explicitly guarantees zero billing, tips, or hidden fees for the service provided, protecting our non-profit status.
  • Unconventional Wish List Attachment: A clipboard ledger attachment that logs donated tools, surplus gear, or materials directly to expand our grassroots field inventory.

2. Mutual Stewardship MOU (Landowners & Nurseries)

Establishing formal partnerships with private timber acreage owners, commercial nurseries, and agricultural sites.

Compliance Framework:

Frames space, storage, or equipment usage as a Matching In-Kind Contribution or Mutual Benefit Exchange rather than "training hours traded for labor," preventing illegal barter classification under federal grant and labor audits.

3. Technical Advisory Board Charter

Defines the structural boundaries for industry professionals and commercial logging partners acting in an advisory capacity.

Governance Protections:

Clarifies the non-voting, advisory-only role of commercial timber partners to maintain strict non-profit board independence, prevent conflict-of-interest violations, and satisfy institutional grant reviewers.
