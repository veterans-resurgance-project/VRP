Operational Track // Outgrowing-Ag

Field Execution & Homestead Partnership Framework

Detailed deployment model pairing disciplined weekend labor crews with senior and legacy homesteads to restore neglected acreage, rebuild soil health, and establish active agricultural mentorship.

1. The Weekend Deployment Model

Structured Weekend Crews

While weekday training focuses on technical tracks and classroom modules, weekends are dedicated exclusively to physical field operations on partner farm sites. Crews operate under strict, pre-written standard operating procedures (SOPs) to ensure self-direction and high safety standards.

  • Site Assessment: OSU Extension collaboration to identify high-need senior/legacy micro-farms.
  • Task Allocation: Pre-assigned work blocks (orchard pruning, bed preparation, composting, irrigation repair).
  • Supervisory Scaling: Floating oversight enabled by rigid checklist-based task execution.

2. Outgrowing-Ag Operational Execution Matrix

Phase / Focus Operational Action Target Outcome
Site Intake Deploy OSU Extension criteria to evaluate legacy homesteads requiring labor support. Signed MOU agreements and established property boundary parameters.
Risk Compliance Post mandatory statutory agritourism warning signs (ORS 30.673) and secure signed waivers. Full legal compliance protecting property owners and program operators.
Field Deployment Execute weekend work blocks utilizing hand tools, drip lines, and soil management gear. Restored agricultural output and practical mentorship exchange.
Review & Scale Evaluate crew efficiency, tool maintenance logs, and landowner feedback. Refined SOPs for expanding site rotations in subsequent terms.
