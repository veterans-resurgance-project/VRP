Vocational Development

Training Tracks & Career Pathways

Military-grade 0800-hour discipline meets high-demand civilian forestry, ecology, and technical field operations.

The "Green Zone" Indoor-to-Field Pipeline

We recognize that putting green, untrained personnel out on rugged, active winter burn scars is a massive safety and insurance liability. That is why our technical training curriculum is structured as a 30-day indoor "Green Zone" training track at our campus footprint during Weeks 1 through 4.

While veterans perform manual seed propagation during morning shifts, afternoon hours are locked into intensive classroom flight school, funding FAA Part 107 commercial licensing exams through localized bridge capital. By Week 5, crews deploy as certified, licensed commercial pilots operating under strict military protocol to gather high-accuracy topographic and thermal research data for regional academic extension programs.

Professional Career Tracks

Wildland Firefighter Type 2 (FFT2)

Training: NWCG modules, physical conditioning, 0800 musters.

Direct qualification for seasonal and permanent wildland firefighting roles with private forestry contractors, state forestry departments, and federal agencies (USFS/BLM).

Timberland Restoration & Forestry Tech

Training: Brush clearance, ladder fuel thinning, chainsaw operations.

Immediate entry-level placement as a forestry technician, logging crew support hand, or timber stand improvement specialist with private timber enterprises.

Riparian & Native Plant Propagation

Training: Riverbank restoration, trail repair, greenhouse seedling propagation.

Employment opportunities with regional nurseries, soil and water conservation districts, watershed councils, and park maintenance divisions.

Remote Emergency Medical & Field Safety

Training: WFA, EMR, CPR certifications, hazard triage protocols.

Stepping stones toward advanced certifications like Wilderness EMT (WEMT) and safety coordinator positions within remote industrial and expedition sectors.

Technical Drone Mapping & GIS Field Tech

Training: Aerial imagery tracking, topography hazard mapping, GIS data.

Entry-level geospatial data collection and drone piloting roles supporting environmental consulting firms, civil engineers, and agricultural management teams.

Crew Supervisor & Site Logistics Lead

Training: Merit-based progression, equipment checkouts, tool accountability.

Promotion into crew foreman, site supervisor, and operational logistics management roles across construction, landscaping, and forestry industries.

Potential Trainee Pathways

Program Career Pathway Examples

Trainee A (Chris): The Ground Sawyer to High-Tech Drone Pilot Loop

  • Spring Training (Stream 1): Chris enrolls in the spring cohort on the grant-sponsored stipend, focusing heavily on ground sawyer felling, chainsaw safety, and wildland fireline construction.
  • Early Summer Community Work (Stream 2): Joins the mobile crew at Waterloo Park for free local wildland fire defense projects, clocking 24-hour clearing windows to protect rural homes.
  • Late Summer Paid Fireline (Stream 3): Promotes to active fire suppression and fuels reduction subcontracts through Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC, earning $24.50/hr on commercial burn zones.
  • Autumn Administrative Gate: As seasonal contracts freeze, Chris hits his contract limits. Because he maintained a flawless safety record on his 100-Point Ledger, he qualifies for the Up-Skilling Loop.
  • Winter Return to Training (Stream 1 Upgrade): Transitions back onto the grant training stipend to upgrade his skills, moving into the winter classroom track to master commercial aerial drone mapping, thermal sensors, and precision burn-scar evaluation.

Trainee B (Marcus): The Hillside Operator to Controlled-Environment Nursery Lead

  • Winter Entry & Propagation (Stream 1): Marcus, managing physical limitations from service-connected injuries, enters the winter module on a grant stipend, focusing on native plant propagation, seed-saving science, and greenhouse management.
  • Spring Community Work (Stream 2): Coordinates logistics for free urban-forestry re-planting projects around Linn County, managing native seedling staging without running heavy hillside machinery.
  • Summer Paid Subcontracts (Stream 3): Cascadia LLC secures a watershed remediation contract; Marcus steps into paid contract work at $24.50/hr, tracking seedling survival rates and soil data.
  • The 250-Hour Transition Gate: After hitting the BOLI/FLSA contract cap, his inventory and scheduling excellence lands him a direct hire by our commercial nursery partner as a permanent, full-time Greenhouse Operations Lead.

Trainee C (Sarah): The Multi-Track Technical Leader

  • Spring Baseline (Stream 1 & 2): Sarah joins the project, quickly passing foundational sawyer safety and basic drone flight hours while serving on free community fuel-reduction crews at Waterloo Park.
  • Summer Field Operations (Stream 3): Transitions to a paid field slot at $24.50/hr. High 100-Point Merit Ledger scores earn her the role of temporary Assistant Crew Lead during multi-acre land clearing subcontracts.
  • Autumn Up-Skilling Re-Entry (Stream 1 Upgrade): After 250 hours of field work, she exercises her re-entry options, moving back into Stream 1 training to combine field knowledge with advanced GIS watershed tracking and drone orthomosaic modeling.
  • Internal Promotion: Utilizes her advanced skills to promote internally into a permanent, commercial revenue-funded role as the Senior Field Lead & UAV Logistics Coordinator for Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC.
