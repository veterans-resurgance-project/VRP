Vocational Pathways

Training Curricula & The Merit Ledger

Detailed breakdown of technical certification tracks, 0800-hour cohort muster standards, and the 100-Point Merit Ledger governing up-skilling progression.

1. Core Vocational Training Tracks (Stream 1)

Ground Sawyer & Wildland Mitigation

Intensive instruction in safe felling, bucking, flash-fuel reduction, and defensible space establishment utilizing professional hand tools, Pulaskis, McLeod tools, and chainsaws under strict safety protocols.

Aerial Drone Mapping & GIS

Commercial thermal and RGB aerial mapping training, flight telemetry logging, offline GIS data collection, and burn-scar ecological evaluation for watershed management.

Greenhouse & Nursery Propagation

Dedicated nursery management tracks focusing on native seed propagation, soil science, and plant survival tracking tailored for disabled veterans.

2. The 100-Point Merit Ledger Evaluation Pillars

To qualify for the up-skilling return loop or advanced leadership tracks, participants are evaluated across four objective benchmarks:

Muster & Discipline (25 Pts) Punctuality at 0800 hours, adherence to chain-of-command protocols, and daily operational readiness.
Field Safety Compliance (35 Pts) Strict PPE utilization, hazard identification, and zero safety infractions during active sweeps.
Technical Execution (25 Pts) Proficiency in tool handling, sawyer techniques, mapping telemetry, or nursery propagation standards.
Peer Leadership (15 Pts) Mentoring junior cohort members, supporting site staging logistics, and fostering unit cohesion.

3. The Up-Skilling Loop Gate

When a participant hits the 250-hour commercial contract limit under Cascadia LLC without immediate outside placement, achieving a minimum score of 90 points on the Merit Ledger with zero safety violations clears them to re-enter Stream 1.

This transitions them back onto the grant-sponsored training stipend strictly to pursue a distinct, higher-tier curriculum milestone (e.g., advancing from Ground Sawyer to Advanced UAV Mapping Pilot), preventing indefinite stagnation.

4. Narrative Examples

🌲 Trainee A (Chris): The Ground Sawyer to High-Tech Drone Pilot Loop

Spring Training (Stream 1): Chris enrolls in the spring cohort, stepping onto the grant-sponsored training stipend. He focuses heavily on ground sawyer felling, heavy chainsaw safety, and wildland firefighter line construction.

Early Summer Community Work (Stream 2): To hone his skills, Chris joins the mobile crew at Waterloo Park for free local wildland fire defense projects, clocking 24-hour clearing windows to protect rural homes.

Late Summer Paid Fireline (Stream 3): When fire season intensifies, Chris promotes to active fire suppression and fuels reduction subcontracts through Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC, earning $24.50/hr on commercial burn zones.

Autumn Administrative Gate: As fire season winds down and seasonal contracts freeze, Chris hits his contract limits. Because he maintained a flawless safety record and zero muster violations on his 100-Point Ledger, he qualifies for the Up-Skilling Loop.
