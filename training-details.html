4. Representative Career Track Examples

These illustrative tracks show how participants can successfully leverage their field hours and technical certifications into high-wage civilian careers:

Track A: Precision Forestry & Logging Tech

Commercial Production Pathway (Track 2A)

Combines advanced chainsaw felling techniques with mechanical harvest operation. Leads directly into competitive positions with regional commercial logging outfits or private timberlands.

Track B: UAS Burn-Scar Mapping Specialist

Drone Telemetry & GIS Pathway

Pairs heavy drone telemetry logging with offline GIS mapping data processing. Prepares graduates for research consulting positions with university extension programs, environmental firms, or forestry services.

Track C: Wildland Wildfire Mitigation Specialist

Emergency & Incident Management Pathway (Track 2B)

Focuses heavily on flash-fuel reduction patterns, emergency logistics, and building defensible spaces. Creates institutional pipelines directly into wildland fire fighting, federal forestry tracks, and state remediation agencies.
