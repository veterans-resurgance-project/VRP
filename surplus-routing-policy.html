Policy Framework

Community Surplus & Asset Routing Policy

← Return to Vault

1. Purpose & Operational Objective

As community support and donations scale across the Willamette Valley, the Veterans Resurgence Project frequently receives high volumes of valuable gear, clothing, and field supplies exceeding immediate cohort requirements. This policy ensures surplus items are immediately deployed to allied missions where they address acute regional needs.

2. Tiered Triage Guidelines

  • Core Retention: Technical forestry equipment, safety PPE, chains, rigging, and active field gear are retained for veteran cohort deployments.
  • Immediate Community Rerouting: Cold-weather essentials—including thermal socks, gloves, blankets, and rain gear—are packaged and routed directly to regional warming shelters and daytime drop-in centers serving unhoused populations.
  • Partner Hand-Offs: General tools or surplus construction materials are transferred to allied rural repair co-ops or local community restoration initiatives.

3. Execution Protocol

Staff will log surplus batches in the internal priority ledger and schedule monthly transfer runs to designated partner agencies, ensuring our storage caches remain lean and mission-focused while multiplying community goodwill.
