The Veterans Resurgence Project

Partnership Categories & Agreement Frameworks

Structured collaboration models ensuring transparency, compliance, and mutual impact across commercial, academic, government, and technical sectors in Linn County.

View Commercial MOU View Waterloo Park Permit

Commercial Partners

Overview: Collaborations with regional logging and forestry contractors that provide heavy machinery access, technical safety guidance, and direct career pipelines.

How the Agreement Works: Operates through a non-financial asset-sharing and trade-for-work framework. Veteran crews perform manual, labor-intensive forestry support (slash piling, hand-line construction, and stream-bank stabilization) in direct exchange for equipment exposure and training. Deployments are bound by a strict 250-hour apprenticeship cap to ensure BOLI compliance and prevent labor displacement.

Appropriate Form/Document: Industrial Operations & Trade-for-Work MOU (including 250-Hour BOLI Compliance Addendum).

Academic Partners

Overview: Non-financial research partnerships with extension services and educational institutions focused on soil science, native plant cultivation, and post-wildfire watershed data collection.

How the Agreement Works: An asset-light data-sharing and resource exchange where project field units provide zero-cost labor for winter greenhouse propagation in return for technical training, soil advisory support, and collaborative drone mapping data.

Appropriate Form/Document: Non-Financial Cooperative Research & Demonstration Agreement.

Government Partners

Overview: Direct coordination with state and local public land management agencies for authorized hazard reduction and public land restoration projects.

How the Agreement Works: Formalized via multi-agency operational MOUs that outline precise liability waivers, equipment-sharing parameters, safety protocols, and designated deployment zones for field units operating in public forests and parks.

Appropriate Form/Document: Public Land Stewardship & Hazard Reduction Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).

Technology & Training Partners

Overview: Educational and technical providers supporting specialized vocational tracks—most notably the 30-day "Green Zone" FAA Part 107 Commercial Drone training curriculum.

How the Agreement Works: Establishes structured training pathways and testing support, pairing military-acquired spatial awareness with licensed commercial UAS operation for post-wildfire slope stability and erosion mapping.

Appropriate Form/Document: Technical Training & Certification Enrollment / Partnership Protocol.
