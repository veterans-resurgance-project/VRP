Community Service & Land Healing

Empowering Veterans, Restoring Ecosystems

The Veterans Resurgence Project operates on a core commitment to free community service, public land restoration, and ecological healing across the Willamette Valley. By deploying trained veteran cohorts to manage hazardous fuels, clean up natural areas, and execute restorative forestry, we serve our region at zero cost to local public partners and landowners. We rely entirely on community allies, fuel card donations, and equipment sponsorships to keep our boots on the ground and our vehicles moving.

Field Intelligence

sUAS Drone Hardware & Starlink Kits

  • Advanced sUAS drone packages for vegetative canopy monitoring and post-wildfire mapping.
  • Portable Starlink Mini kits for live telemetry and remote field communications.

Regulatory Safety

OSHA PPE, Trauma Kits & MSDS Binders

  • Forestry helmets, chaps, steel-toe logger boots, and high-vis vests.
  • Class A/B industrial first aid stations and individual trauma/bleed control packs.
  • Waterproof 3-ring binders for Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) and JHA logsheets.
