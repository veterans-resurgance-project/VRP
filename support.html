Community Support & Wish List

The Veteran Resurgence Project Wish List

Every contribution—whether it's spare workshop gear sitting in your garage or fuel assistance for our field crews—directly supports post-wildfire watershed restoration and agricultural training across Linn County and the Willamette Valley.

Tier 1: Everyday Workshop & Field Supplies

Small Items You Might Have Around the House

Building community momentum starts with small, accessible contributions. If you have any clean, gently used, or extra items lying around, your donation keeps our restoration and training work moving forward:

  • Work Gloves & Safety Glasses: Durable leather work gloves or clear eye protection for field days.
  • Hand Tools: Extra hand trowels, pruners, loppers, and sturdy hammers.
  • Storage & Organization: Clean 5-gallon buckets with lids, plastic storage bins, and heavy-duty tarps.
  • Hydration Gear: Clean coolers, insulated thermoses, and reusable water jugs for work crews.
Tier 2: Field Logistics & Travel

Transportation & Field Fuel Assistance

Transporting trainees, equipment, and restoration materials across Linn County requires dependable transportation support:

  • Fuel & Gas Cards: Gift cards or direct contributions for travel to rural project sites and state forest restoration lanes.
  • Vehicle Maintenance Support: Assistance with routine maintenance, tire checks, and hauling equipment.
Tier 3: Program Expansion

Advanced Equipment & Enterprise Tools

For major contributors or corporate partners looking to fund heavier infrastructure, we maintain a registry of commercial-grade agricultural tools, irrigation supplies, and native plant propagation materials.
