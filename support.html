How You Can Help

Partner with our disciplined veteran field cohorts through direct in-kind equipment contributions or schedule community protection service tiers.

Asset-Light Mandate

In-Kind Gear & Tool Contributions

To respect our strict asset-light operational policies and prevent early equipment debt, we prioritize direct tool and gear contributions over cash. Donors can supply certified safety gear, professional Pulaskis, or spill-proof fuel lockers directly to our Waterloo Park operations hub.

Free Community Tier

Landowner Access & Service Requests

Property owners can apply for our 16-to-24 hour free community service tier. Our trained veteran squads execute hazardous brush reduction, ladder fuel thinning, and wildland fire defense clearing at zero cost under strict landowner waiver agreements.
