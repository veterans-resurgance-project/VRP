How You Can Help & What We Do

Bridging veteran reintegration with vital ecological health across the Willamette Valley through structured community service, transparent compliance, and skilled stewardship.

Mission & Operational Structure

Operating under a disciplined 0800-hour morning muster, our veteran cohorts restore public and private watersheds through hands-on land management. By combining military-grade structure with professional environmental stewardship, we heal ecosystems while rebuilding purposeful civilian careers for veterans.

The Three Operational Streams

  • Stream 1 (Training & Stipend): Grant-funded classroom instruction, sawyer certifications, and UAV drone mapping operations. (Zero commercial timber harvesting).
  • Stream 2 (Free Community Work): 16–24 hour capped defensible space clearing and roadside fuel reduction under the non-profit banner, backed by our "No Surcharges" Declaration.
  • Stream 3 (Paid Commercial Contracts): Large-scale commercial units and heavy acreage prep transitioning into Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC at a competitive W-2 wage of $24.50/hr (capped at 250 hours).

What We Can Do For You (Services & Examples)

We deliver targeted, high-impact land management services structured cleanly around our non-profit community windows and commercial subcontracts:

Defensible Space & Flash-Fuel Reduction (Free Window) Clearing dead brush, ladder fuels, and low-hanging limbs within the 30-to-100 foot safety perimeter around rural residential homes or local community zones.
Rapid Roadside Fuel Reduction (Free Window) Clearing heavy overgrowth along narrow private driveways and rural access routes to ensure clear ingress and egress for emergency vehicles.
Commercial Acreage & Heavy Mitigation (Paid Subcontract) Large parcel prep, complex slash piling, and post-wildfire erosion control managed under Cascadia LLC commercial subcontracts.

How You Can Support the Mission

We don't just accept standard funding—we build our operational capacity through grassroots equipment matching and community engagement:

  • The Unconventional Wish List (In-Kind Gear): If you have surplus gear, working chainsaws, hand tools, or transport trailers, we log them via our clipboard ledger as approved In-Kind contributions to expand our field inventory organically.
  • Financial Contributions & Grants: Donations directly fund safety equipment (PPE), technical certification testing fees, and daily logistics for our veteran cohorts as they transition into skilled ecological careers.
