Watership Stewards Empowering

Restoring landscapes, renewing purpose.

The Mission

This pilot program supports veterans through community, hands-on watershed restoration, seed propagation, and targeted training. By anchoring recovery in tangible, life-giving environmental stewardship, participants help heal post-wildfire ecosystems across the Willamette Valley while reclaiming a lasting sense of purpose.
