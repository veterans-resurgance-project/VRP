Mission & Impact Overview

Restoring Land, Rebuilding Lives

The Veterans Resurgence Project (VRP), operating in powerful alignment with Watership Stewards and Cascadia Forestry Solutions LLC, establishes a vital bridge between veteran vocational reintegration and essential regional land stewardship across the Willamette Valley. By anchoring our daily operations in Linn County, we have engineered a multi-tiered model that transforms military discipline into high-demand civilian careers while delivering profound, tangible benefits to our local communities and ecosystems.

Community Stimulus

Providing critical, no-cost wildfire defense and defensible space clearing for vulnerable rural properties at sites like Waterloo Park, shielding local infrastructure from fire risks.

Watershed Protection

Tackling heavy post-wildfire remediation, ladder fuel thinning, and riparian zone rehabilitation, systematically restoring cleared areas with native vegetation to safeguard regional waterways.

What truly sets the VRP apart is its rigorous Educational Integration and Technical Training Pipeline. We combine traditional ground-sawyer field work with cutting-edge academic partnerships, integrating regional university extension services for drone-based topographic data logging, thermal imaging, and GIS research. Veterans transition seamlessly from introductory stipends into high-paying commercial subcontracts ($24.50/hr) and advanced technical certifications like FAA Part 107 drone piloting, Wilderness First Aid, and greenhouse operations management.

Through our unique 100-Point Merit Ledger and structured up-skilling loops, participants are equipped not just with temporary work, but with sustainable, long-term career pathways—whether they promote internally into leadership roles with Cascadia or step into permanent positions with regional forestry and environmental enterprises.
