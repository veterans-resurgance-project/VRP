The Veteran Resurgence Project

Healing Ecosystems. Rebuilding Lives.

The Veterans Resurgence Project bridges the gap between post-military transition and environmental stewardship. Operating under a disciplined 0800-hour cohort model, we deploy veteran teams to restore fire-damaged public and private watersheds across Linn County and the Willamette Valley. By combining hands-on wildland mitigation with advanced technical certifications, we build resilient ecosystems while launching veterans into lifelong, sustainable natural resource careers.

Core Training Tracks & Vocational Pathways

Structured curriculum tiers guiding participants from foundational military discipline to advanced ecological leadership.

Ground Sawyer & Wildland Mitigation

Intensive felling, bucking, flash-fuel reduction, and defensible space establishment utilizing professional Pulaskis, McLeod tools, and chainsaws.

Aerial Drone Mapping & GIS

Commercial thermal and RGB aerial mapping training, flight telemetry logging, offline GIS data collection, and burn-scar ecological evaluation.

Greenhouse & Nursery Propagation

Dedicated greenhouse management tracks focusing on native seed propagation, soil science, and plant survival tracking for disabled veterans.

Crew Leadership & Logistics

Advanced administrative training covering site staging, chain-of-command protocols, and 100-Point Merit Ledger tracking benchmarks.
