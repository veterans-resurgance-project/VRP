Veterans Resurgence Project

A structured, W-2 workforce restoring regional watersheds and reducing hazardous fuels, while building career-track skills in ecology, native plant propagation, and forestry. Integrating FAA Part 107 GIS monitoring and science-based education.

Forest & Watershed Restoration

Structured veteran crews conduct intensive, labor-intensive hazardous fuels reduction, manual slash piling, hand-line construction, and post-wildfire reforestation to enhance regional ecosystem health.

Native Plant Nursery & Propagation

Scaling up capacity for regional riparian and habitat recovery projects. We cultivate a wide variety of native Willamette Valley species for restoration efforts and community distribution.

Academic Research
Aerial Mapping

Comprehensive training track for FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot licensing, utilizing aerial thermal imaging for terrain assessment, post-fire watershed analysis, and vegetation health monitoring.

Operational Framework & Training

A fully integrated, W-2 workforce model combining structured field operations with formal, verifiable educational pathways in soil science, botany, and pest management.

The Waterloo Park Operation Hub

Operating out of our centralized headquarters in Linn County, Oregon, our mission bridges the gap between veteran vocational rehabilitation and critical regional land stewardship. Through strict morning musters, asset-light operational frameworks, and robust university partnerships, we turn wilderness defense into stable civilian pathways.

0800 Safety Musters

Structured military-style accountability, real-time hazard briefs, and 100-Point Merit tracking.

Asset-Light Mandate

Zero early equipment debt; heavy operations integrated via direct timber industry subcontracts.
