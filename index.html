Roots of Support, Fruits of Impact

Every successful initiative relies on a deeply integrated ecosystem. Just like a mature tree, our work draws strength from foundational roots, rises through structural core infrastructure, and yields tangible, sustainable outcomes for our community.

Outcomes

Sustainable Branches & Fruits (Tangible Outcomes)

Watershed Defense

Mitigating topsoil erosion, protecting Willamette Valley waterways, and preventing mudslides.

Timber Recovery

Unlocking salvage timber from rot, clearing stream-clogging debris, and supporting local nurseries.

Veteran Careers

Providing stable employment, purpose-driven missions, and long-term career placement.

Regional Resilience

Long-term ecological security, data-backed restoration, and community-wide recovery.
