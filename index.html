Restoring Our Landscape, Empowering Our Veterans

The Veteran Resurgence Project unites military discipline with environmental stewardship. Across Linn County and the Willamette Valley, our dedicated crews work year-round to heal fire-damaged watersheds, mitigate topsoil erosion, and provide valuable research data through strategic collaborations with private and academic partners—transforming critical recovery missions into stable, purpose-driven careers for those who have served.

Why This Work Matters & Partnership Framework

Post-wildfire recovery, watershed management, and veteran re-integration require disciplined, structural intervention that bridges ecological defense with human stability.

Ecosystem Defense & Remediation

Protecting Willamette Valley waterways from silt runoff, stabilizing fire-weakened slopes, mitigating topsoil erosion, and ensuring long-term native forest health through active watershed stewardship.

The Balanced Housing Bridge Model

Replacing solitary re-entry efforts with 24/7 team accountability, mission-focused environmental targets, and verified vocational training tailored for unhoused and underemployed veterans in Linn County.

Collaborations

Formal Partnership Agreements & Field Protocols

The Veterans Resurgence Project establishes collaborative field-training partnerships through formal Memorandums of Understanding (MOU) with regional private logging and timber firms. These agreements provide veteran cohorts with commercial site access for watershed restoration and fuel reduction while offering partner firms pre-screened labor at zero overhead.

Technical Advisory Boards

Oversight from state and private forestry specialists ensuring compliance with ODF guidelines.

Nursery & Academic Pipelines

Joint native plant propagation initiatives integrated with regional extension networks.

Social Support Referrals

Warm-referral pipelines coordinating local health, housing, and recovery resources.
