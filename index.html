Explore Our Model

Roots of Support, Fruits of Impact

Every successful initiative relies on a deeply integrated ecosystem. Just like a mature tree, our work draws strength from foundational roots—our veteran alliances, municipal partners, and mechanical trade networks that invest in our mission.

As that vital support rises through the branches, it manifests as public recognition, safety compliance, and sustainable growth, ultimately bearing tangible outcomes for our community.

Operational Wish List

Support our field operations in Linn County by helping us equip our crews with essential restoration tools, safety gear, and logistical hardware.

Field Tools

Pulaskis, mattocks, hand saws, and heavy-duty shovels for trail and slope stabilization.

Soil Testing Kits

Advanced chemical and nutrient testing kits to evaluate burn severity and recovery viability.

Safety Equipment (PPE)

High-visibility gear, helmets, durable boots, and eye protection for field personnel.

Drone Hardware

Aerial mapping drones to assess hard-to-reach watershed damage and erosion patterns.

Transport Trailers

Utility trailers for hauling gear, tools, and native planting materials to remote sites.

Communications Gear

Rugged two-way radios and field comms for remote operations where cell service fails.
